# Trestleboard

Twenty-four practices borrowed from old traditions, *A Field Guide to the Western Mysteries*, and a log of what works for you, in one app for your phone.

Everything in it comes from free or public-domain sources.

## Putting it on an iPhone

1. Open this app's GitHub Pages link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Open it once with internet so the phone saves its copy. After that it opens with no signal.

## What's in it

- **Practices**: a short welcome the first time you open it, the line of the day, "How are you right now?" with eight groups (Starting, Foggy, Too Much, Heated, Looping, Deciding, People, Winding Down), three routines, a quiet timer, and all 24 practices with their tools (breathing guides, timers, voice guidance, background sounds, and a dim screen for bedtime). The line of the day also shows the day's planet, metal, and a lens for the day.
- **For steady moments**: 18 small practices for ordinary hours, sorted by length (seconds, about a minute, a few minutes), and the Watch, a calm page to leave open all day, with bells (every so many minutes or at the old monastic hours), one small practice at a time, a quick check-in, one habit to count, and a slow drift through the app's own reading. It can read aloud and has a plain look for shared screens.
- **Read**: About (the preface, "Where the Threads Cross," and "Reading the Fine Print"); the Field Guide (all of *A Field Guide to the Western Mysteries*); and Extras (one combined glossary and the Free Shelf).
- **My Log**: your before-and-after ratings, parked thoughts, your check-ins by time of day, shadow notes, and a backup button. The evening review (Raising the Sparks) also shows what you saved that day.
- **Voices**: two built-in voices, Heart and Michael, so guidance sounds the same on a phone or a computer and works offline. You can also use your device's own voice.

Your ratings, notes, and pins are saved only on your phone. They are never uploaded here.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app
- `sw.js`: lets the phone keep a copy so it works offline
- `voices/`: the built-in voice recordings, one file per voice. They were made with [Kokoro](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), a free, open-source voice (Apache 2.0 license).
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/`: the home-screen name and icon
