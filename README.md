# Trestleboard

Twenty-four practices borrowed from old traditions, a breathing companion called the Watch, and *A Field Guide to the Western Mysteries*, in one app for your phone or computer.

Everything in it comes from free or public-domain sources.

## Putting it on an iPhone

1. Open this app's GitHub Pages link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

Open it once with internet so the phone saves its copy. After that it opens with no signal.

## What's in it

- **Home**: a short welcome each time you open or come back to the app (with a tour of the tabs the first time), the Watch, today's question (from the day's planet, metal, and a principle from *The Kybalion*), a line for today with the practice that goes with it, "When something snags" with eight groups (Inertia, Low energy, Overload, Reactivity, Rumination, Indecision, Friction, Transitions).
- **Watch**: a breathing companion to leave running in the background like a video. Pick one or more of four breathing patterns from the practices (slow breathing, a long exhale, box breathing, or the double-inhale sigh), or a mix of them, press Start, and it breathes with you set after set, with short rests between, until you pause or stop it. While each pattern runs, it shows where the pattern comes from and why it might help. Underneath, it can play any blend of soft music (in four moods: Warm, Dusk, Open, and Piano), breath tones, a low drone, rain, ocean, wind, fire, a thunderstorm, crickets, brown noise, and pink noise, each with its own volume (the background sounds matched so they sound about equally loud), and all made by the app itself. Four ready-made scenes (Rain study, Hearth, Night porch, Shoreline) set a blend in one tap, and you can save your own. While the Watch runs, the music's tone opens and closes a little with your breath. It's the same mix as the background sound in the top bar's sound settings, so changing one changes the other. It can keep the screen on while it runs, and dim it. It keeps running while you move to other pages, with a slim bar above the tabs that shows a small breathing shape, what to do now, and its own pause button (breathing waits while a practice's own tool is running, or while a routine or the timer is on screen; the app has to stay open, and on a phone the screen has to stay on).
- **Practices**: where to start, then all 24 practices by group, with one in each group marked "Start here," each with its tools (breathing guides, timers, practice tones that match the soft music's chord while it plays, background sounds and soft music, and a dim screen for bedtime); three routines (Settle in, Reset, Close out); and a quiet timer. The evening review (Raising the Sparks) also shows what you saved that day.
- **Read**: About (the preface, "Where the Threads Cross," and "Reading the Fine Print"); the Field Guide (all of *A Field Guide to the Western Mysteries*); and Extras (one combined glossary, where each word lists the pages that use it, and the Free Shelf). Glossary words are underlined in the reading and the practices; tap one for its definition. Each reading page remembers where you left off, and the Read tab links back to the last one. The sound settings are there too, so music or rain can play while you read.
- **Aa**: on every page, text size (for the whole app) and light or dark (or match the device).

Your notes, pins, and settings are saved only on the device you use them on. They are never uploaded here.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app
- `sw.js`: lets the phone keep a copy so it works offline
- `manifest.webmanifest` and `icons/`: the home-screen name and icon
